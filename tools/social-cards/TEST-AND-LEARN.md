# Test and learn: how the social rhythm finds what resonates

Kristian, 01/11/2026: "Have more researching going on a weekly basis together with the analytics
... what is resonating ... where we're getting the most views and engagement, and we hit on that
one and refine that and make more content like it ... try different formats once in a while ... it
might just be a fluke one day that one thing hit ... make sure that it's continuous to check and
improve and see what hits."

## The loop (every Sunday and Wednesday run)

1. **Measure.** Pull every sent post's metrics. Score each post as a LIFT: its impressions divided
   by its channel's median over the last 28 days (so a quiet channel and a loud one compare fairly).
   Engagement rate (reactions + real comments + shares, over impressions) is the second score.
2. **Research.** Scout the week's trends (trends-*.md), and on Sundays also look at what the
   best-performing posts in our niche did this week (AI agents, MCP, Claude, AI for small business,
   solo founders): their hook, format and picture. Note any format we have not tried.
3. **Decide with an evidence bar.** A post with lift >= 2 is a CANDIDATE winner, never a winner.
4. **Replicate before believing.** A candidate's angle, layout or hook style is re-run in a NEW form
   (new text, new picture, a different weekday and slot) within the next 2 weeks. It becomes a
   CONFIRMED winner only if the replication also beats the channel median (lift >= 1.3). If the
   replication flops it was a fluke: mark it RETIRED as a fluke and say so.
5. **Exploit, refine, explore.** Each brand page's 6 posts a week:
   - 3 EXPLOIT: confirmed winners (or, until we have any, the best candidates), in new forms.
   - 2 REFINE: one change to a winner (a new hook style, a different layout for the same idea, a
     trending topic in a winning layout). Change one thing at a time so we know what moved it.
   - 1 EXPLORE: something we have not tried (a new layout, a new topic area, a new hook type, a
     new time). Every explore is logged as an experiment with its hypothesis.
6. **Log it.** Every experiment and every candidate lives in learnings/EXPERIMENTS.md with: id,
   channel, hypothesis, what was changed, posts (date and key), lift, status (running, candidate,
   confirmed, fluke, retired). A confirmed winner stays confirmed only while its last 3 runs average
   lift >= 1.0; otherwise it decays to "tired" and drops to refine.

Day and slot are never welded to a format (the weekly rotation), so a weekday effect cannot pass
for a format effect. Findings about formats, layouts, days and slots still need n >= 8 and a
median 1.5x away from the overall median before they change the plan.
